York-Durham Heritage Railway · October 2019

Multi-Sensor Field Dataset for Autonomous Rail

Field data collection for object detection on rail: a camera, a Cepton LiDAR and radar mounted on a cart and run along a heritage railway across seven tests of two to four runs each.

  • Extracted frames at 10 Hz with ffmpeg and converted each recording timestamp to Unix time, which is what let the camera, LiDAR and radar streams be lined up at all.
  • Cut the raw LiDAR into 0.1-second scenes so every scene had exactly one frame to match, then solved the per-test time offset and the camera intrinsics and extrinsics by hand — the delivered data was missing mounting geometry — and checked the alignment against rendered depth maps.
  • Hand-labelled roughly 19,000 bounding boxes with a colleague across six classes (person, car, train, box, bicycle, switch position indicator), exported in both YOLO and PascalVOC layouts.
